Traits of Early Computers

  1. Very Large: Early computers often filled entire rooms.
  2. Extremely Expensive: They cost millions to build and maintain.
  3. Slow Processing: Capable of only a few thousand operations per second.

Historical Highlights

  • ENIAC (USA, 1945–46): First general-purpose electronic computer.
  • Colossus (UK, 1943–44): Used to break German encryption during WWII.
  • UNIVAC I (1951): First commercially available computer.
